\begin{abstract}
This study develops a multi-stage, hybrid yield forecasting system designed to bridge the gap between process-based interpolation and machine-learning extrapolation. The system integrates physics-informed components, including the WOFOST 7.2 simulation and a Multivariate Heat Signal model. The final forecast is generated by a Super Ensemble which uses a Meta-Learner to dynamically assign probability-based weights to component models via soft voting. Validated via strict time-dependent cross-validation, the Super Ensemble achieved a Mean Absolute Error (MAE) of 56.28 dt/ha over the 2000--2024 period, representing a 16.27\% improvement in skill over the statistical trend baseline.
\end{abstract}
