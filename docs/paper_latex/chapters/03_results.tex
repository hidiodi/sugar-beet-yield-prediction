\subsection{Anomaly Forensics}
The model's resilience is most evident during extreme anomalies where the \textbf{Statistical Trend} baseline typically fails due to regime blindness. In the historic heatwave of 2003 (actual yield 516.9 dt/ha), the Super Ensemble successfully identified the stress regime via the Multivariate Heat Signal ($M_1$), which achieved an operational correlation of $r=0.63$ between the March 1st forecast and observed August heat days. This directional signal allowed the model to predict 581.8 dt/ha (MAE 80.7), significantly outperforming the \textbf{Statistical Trend} (MAE 95.8).

The system's most critical impact occurred during the 2018 drought (actual 628.5 dt/ha). While the \textbf{Statistical Trend} over-predicted yield at 799.7 dt/ha (MAE 182.1), the Super Ensemble pivoted to physics-informed components, predicting 710.3 dt/ha. This represents a 43.1\% reduction in absolute error. Conversely, in the 2014 bumper year (actual 825.6 dt/ha), the system successfully signaled a directional shift toward a high-yield regime by predicting 748.6 dt/ha. While this remains an under-prediction of the absolute peak yield—consistent with the inherent conservatism of ensemble-based machine learning—it represents a meaningful improvement over the \textbf{Statistical Trend} forecast of 715.9 dt/ha, which remained stagnant at the long-term average.

The core strength of the Super Ensemble is its ability to eliminate regime blindness, achieving \textbf{483 hits on True Crash} regimes. The Regret-Weighted training prioritized these extremes: the probability weight assigned to physics-informed sub-models ($M_1$, $M_3$) in 2018 was significantly higher ($>0.75$) than in the stable 2010--2013 period ($<0.15$), where the \textbf{Statistical Trend} was preferred.

\subsection{Ablation Study}
Ablation results (Fig~\ref{fig:ablation}) confirm that the removal of bio-physical stress signals (CASDI, NMSD, OSAW) causes the sharpest performance decline during extreme years, increasing 2018 error by over 15\%. Notably, the exclusion of the non-climatic outlier detection filter resulted in a negligible performance change ($<0.5\%$ skill), proving that the results are driven by the architectural integration of physical mechanisms rather than data selection.
