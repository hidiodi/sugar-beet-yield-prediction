\subsection{Dynamics and Ablation}
Forensic analysis confirms that the $S_{hybrid}$ signal effectively modulates the regime choice. In the 2018 drought, the signal correctly identified a deviation from the technological trend without triggering the extreme "Safety Mode" (which would have reverted to the Trend). Instead, the system entered the "Normal Regime," activating a blend of the Robust Linear ($M_2$) and Bio-Physical Limits ($M_3$) models. This blend reduced the prediction error by \textbf{58.4 dt/ha} compared to the trend baseline. The Expert System strategy achieved an overall MAE of 61.79~dt/ha, outperforming a "Hard Switching" approach (MAE 69.19~dt/ha).

Systemic component failure accounts for 65.7\% of the remaining error, while selection error (sub-optimal regime assignment) represents only 34.3\% of the total residual. Ablation results (Fig~\ref{fig:ablation}) confirm that removing bio-physical stress signals from the integration logic increases 2018 drought error significantly, while the exclusion of the outlier filter changed overall skill by $<0.1\%$.
