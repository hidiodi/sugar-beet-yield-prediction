\subsection{Anomaly Forensics}
The model's resilience is particularly evident during extreme anomalies, where standard statistical approaches fail to extrapolate. In the historic heatwave of 2003 (actual yield 516.9 dt/ha), the Super Ensemble successfully identified the heat stress signal despite SEAS5 uncertainty. Notably, the Heat Signal component achieved a correlation of \textbf{$r=0.56$} between the March 1st forecast and observed August heat days. This strong directional signal allowed the model to predict a reduced yield of 581.9 dt/ha (MAE 80.7), significantly outperforming the Trend model (MAE 95.8).

The value of this approach was most critical during the severe drought of 2018 (actual 628.5 dt/ha). While the Statistical Trend model failed to capture the downturn, resulting in a massive MAE of 182.1 dt/ha, the Super Ensemble limited the error to 104.7 dt/ha. This represents a \textbf{42.5\% reduction in error} during the decade's most critical failure event, confirming the system's ability to act as a reliable early warning mechanism. Conversely, in the bumper year of 2014 (actual 825.6 dt/ha), the system correctly captured the upside potential, predicting 749.5 dt/ha against the conservative Trend forecast of 715.9 dt/ha.

\subsection{Regime Switching Dynamics}
The core strength of the Super Ensemble is its ability to switch strategies based on the detected environmental context. Figure \ref{fig:regime_maps} illustrates this spatial decision-making process. In 2014, the Meta-Learner favored the Trend model in the substantial majority of districts, leveraging stable conditions to minimize variance. However, during the 2018 drought, the system shifted decisively to the Physics-Informed components, with the Heat Signal and Hybrid XGB models dominating the weight distribution in the worst-hit regions of Northern and Eastern Germany.

\subsection{Ablation Study}
To quantify the contribution of specific components, we performed an ablation study (Figure \ref{fig:ablation}) by systematically removing key modules. The removal of bio-physical stress signals (e.g., $I_{\text{scorch}}$, $I_{\text{waterlog}}$) caused the sharpest performance decline during extreme years, with the MAE for 2018 increasing by over 15\%. This confirms that standard weather variables alone are insufficient for capturing physiological thresholds.

Furthermore, replacing the dynamic gating with a static ``Equal Weight'' ensemble reduced the global $R^2$ from 0.618 to 0.552, demonstrating that the regime-switching logic adds significant value beyond simple model averaging. Finally, we tested the model trained on the full dataset versus the cleaned dataset (excluding $\epsilon_{\text{oracle}} > 200$ dt/ha). The inclusion or exclusion of this filter resulted in a negligible performance difference ($< 0.5\%$ change in Skill Score), empirically validating that the results are driven by the physics-informed architecture rather than data filtering.
